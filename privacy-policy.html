Privacy Policy

Little Pilot: Sky Adventures · Last updated November 25, 2026

Little Pilot collects nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from anyone who uses it — children or adults.

No data collection

Little Pilot has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, usage analytics, or any other data. There is no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising software of any kind built into the app.

What stays on your device

The game saves its progress (worlds visited, missions finished, planes chosen) and any pilot names a child types in, so they are there next time. All of this is stored only on the device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when the app is deleted.

The game works offline

Little Pilot is a fully offline game. It does not connect to the internet to play, and it sends no information to us or to any server.

Play Together (local only)

The optional Play Together mode lets two devices fly together over the same local Wi‑Fi network, connecting directly device‑to‑device using Apple’s built‑in local networking. Only game information (plane positions, button presses and the pilot’s name and plane) passes between the two devices during a game. Nothing is sent over the internet, nothing is sent to us, and nothing is stored.

Purchases

Little Pilot offers a single one‑time purchase to unlock all worlds. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, behind a grown‑ups check. We never see or receive your payment information. Purchase details are managed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

Little Pilot is designed to be safe for children and is intended for the Kids Category of the App Store. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. It contains no advertising and no social features. The only ways out of the game (buying the unlock, and emailing us from the For Grown‑Ups panel) sit behind a grown‑ups check. This is consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the App Store’s requirements for apps made for kids.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new “last updated” date.
